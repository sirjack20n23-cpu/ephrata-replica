# 08 — Parks, leisure, tourism & landmarks

## Parks & leisure
- Parks raise attractiveness / help buildings level and can improve budget via taxes. [S01]
- Spread small parks for coverage; large plazas/stadiums are destination magnets (Timeister sports multiplex as tourist draw). [S18]
- Too many parks in one neighborhood can push land value/rent up. [S25]

## Tourism
- Hotels + attractions + good access (parking or transit) work together. [S18][S23]
- Landmarks (unique/tourist landmarks) concentrate tourists — Barbosa places famous landmarks as visual anchors in vanilla cities. [S32]
- CPP tourist-town approach: leisure assets, hotels, parks, services, then reshape grid to fit them. [S02]
- Seasonal recreation (e.g. water parks) can boost specific seasons — treat as optional flair. [S20]

## Creative placement
- Pair waterfront marinas / recreation with landscaping, not only parking lots (Keralis viewers pushed for trees/bushes). [S22]
- Signature buildings (diners near industry, plazas downtown) sell the story of the district. [S16]

## Claude checklist
1. Fix basic services and budget before a tourism spending spree.
2. For Ephrata: small-town parks and Main Street landmarks > mega theme parks unless user wants fantasy.
3. Ask before placing huge unique buildings that lock land.
