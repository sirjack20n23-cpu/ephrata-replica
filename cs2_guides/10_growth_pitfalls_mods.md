# 10 — Growth phases, pitfalls & mods

## Early game
- Power + water + sewage before serious zoning. [S01]
- Do not buy every adjacent tile immediately — expensive and spreads services thin. [S17]
- Stabilize budget before massive specialized industry. [S05]
- ImperialJedi-style first 30 minutes: get basics running, grow density carefully, watch noise. [S36]

## Mid game
- Add education ladder before office/high-tech dreams. [S25]
- Introduce buses, then trams as density rises. [S04][S15]
- Build cargo solutions when truck spam starts — not only more lanes. [S11]
- Expand industry and housing together so jobs/homes stay balanced. [S11][S01]

## Late game
- Metro/train/airport are costly; justify with ridership and land. [S15][S23]
- Fix electricity distribution (transformers), not only generation. [S31]
- Tourism and landmarks last after the city works. [S02][S32]

## Common pitfalls
| Pitfall | Better move | Source |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Spamming industry zoning | Build resource-based chains + access | [S05][S11] |
| Locals onto arterials/highways | Hierarchy + collectors | TRAFFIC |
| Highway into roundabout | Proper ramps / interchange | TRAFFIC [S04] |
| Ignoring demand bars | Zone to each residential density bar | [S01] |
| High rent panic demolish | Education, jobs, low-rent zones | [S25] |
| One transformer for huge plant | Multiple transformers / paths | [S31] |
| Sewage upstream of intake | Downstream outlet / treatment | [S28] |
| Unconnected cargo rail | Link to outside network | TRAFFIC |
| Max taxes forever | Moderate taxes + local production | [S01][S24] |
