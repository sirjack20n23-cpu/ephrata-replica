# 04 — Public transit

## Hierarchy — pick the right mode
| Mode | Role | Notes | Sources |
|------|------|-------|---------|
| Taxi | Flexible last-mile | Low capacity; adds road traffic; **local taxis need a taxi depot** (outside taxis dropping off ≠ your service) | [S04] |
| Bus | Local / express backbone | Cheap, flexible; depot in accessible low-traffic spot; unlock ~Grand Village era | [S04][S15] |
| Tram | Medium-density corridors | Needs tracks + tram depot; great with mixed-use / streetcar suburbs | [S04][S08] |
| Metro | High capacity, off-street | When buses/trams cannot keep up; stations at transfer points; can raise land value | [S15] |
| Train | District / regional | Longer trips across the map | [S15][S04] |
| Ferry | Water links | Map-dependent | — |
| Airport | Late specialty | Huge footprint; stage carefully | [S23] |

Link modes at hubs; do not run duplicative parallel services on the same corridor. [S04][S15]

## Buses (first network)
- Build a bus depot first; place it where buses can exit without jamming a main street. [S15]
- Connect residential ↔ workplaces ↔ schools ↔ commercial. [S15][S04]
- Stop spacing: walkable, not stop-every-door. [S04]
- Too many buses on one arterial recreates congestion — add capacity modes or bus lanes (user may need to upgrade roads). [S04][TRAFFIC]
- ConflictNerd: put stops at train-station doors; electric buses cut suburban noise. [S27]
- Fare-free can boost ridership when fixing car traffic (CPP). [TRAFFIC]

## Trams
- Use for denser corridors and streetcar-suburb patterns. [S08]
- Trams cutting through roundabouts conflict — choose one at a junction. [TRAFFIC]
- Leave land for a future tram terminal / depot expansion. [S21][S15]

## Metro & rail
- Metro does not share road space — plan station catchment and transfers to bus/tram/train. [S15]
- Poorly planned metro is costly; plan lines before digging. [S15]
- Always leave expansion room for stations and yards. [S15]

## Airports & campuses
- Airports need highway/cargo thinking and lots of land. [S23]
- Campus/university districts need transit, not only parking. [S23]

## Claude checklist
1. Survey lines, waiting cims, and traffic before adding a mode.
2. Propose the next hierarchy step with land + money needs.
3. Ask user for ticket prices, vehicle counts, and any depot tweaks you cannot set.
4. Ephrata: bus along Main/State first; reserve a future rail/tram corridor if replicating regional links.
