# 06 — City services

## Coverage mindset
- Place services for **response coverage and road access**, not only pretty plazas.
- Early: healthcare clinic, fire, police as population grows; do not bankrupt the city by plopping everything at once. [S01][S17]

## Education & skilled labor
- Elementary → high school → college/university as eligible students grow. [S01]
- "Not enough highly educated workers": check building employee education mix; bump **education budget** slightly for mild gaps; for serious gaps, open Education info and add capacity where eligible students exceed seats. [S25]
- Universities are large signature moves — stage with housing and transit (Campus content). [S23]

## Healthcare & deathcare
- Clinics first; hospitals later. Deathcare (cemetery/crematorium) before bodies backlog. [S01]
- Healthcare buildings want decent road access for ambulances.

## Police & fire
- Cover industrial and dense areas carefully (higher incident risk). Boat police exist for waterfront maps. [S23]
- Avoid stacking so many service buildings that land value / rent spikes painfully in working-class areas. [S25]

## Claude checklist
1. Survey coverage gaps and building complaints.
2. Fix education labor shortages before demolishing "broken" industry.
3. Ask before placing university-scale campuses or deleting schools.
