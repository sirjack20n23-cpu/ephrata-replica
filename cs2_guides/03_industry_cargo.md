# 03 — Industry, specialized industry & cargo

## Core rule (Economy 2.0 thinking)
Local resources and manufacturing make goods cheaper, raise factory profits, enrich citizens, and help buildings level — better than endless imports. [S01]

## Do not spam industrial zones
- CPP: stop painting huge empty industrial fields; build a **story-backed** chain (resource → processing → distribution) with proper roads/rail first. [S05]
- Fix the industrial road/rail layout before stacking more specialized industry. [S05]
- Employee shortages are a top failure mode — add housing, transit, and education before blaming the extractor. [S05]

## Specialized industry
- Place extractors on real resource overlays (forestry, grain, livestock, ore, oil, fishing, etc.). [S01][S05]
- CPP example pattern: timber by forest + rail, then processing nearby; unlock supply-chain buildings as they appear. [S05]
- Keralis: farms need large land and rural, bendy roads; keep scale honest vs downtown. [S22]
- NunoLac fishing: profitability tracks resource quality — buy better fishing tiles when expanding; open-water fishing can improve once you hold the right water. [S29]
- two dollars twenty: heavy industry wants strong rail to raw resources plus storage/generic industry belts. [S19]

## Manufacturing vs warehouses
- Zoned industry can spawn factories **or** warehouses. Warehouses alone will not fix cargo chaos. [S11]
- Biffa: sometimes **adding more manufacturing industry** reduces long truck hauls by producing locally — test after surveying truck destinations. [S11]
- Pair industry parks with cargo rail / transfer stations so trucks are not crossing the whole city. [S11]

## Industrial road design
- T4rget: multi-purpose industrial layouts with clear main access, priority merges into the site, dock/harbor adjacency when relevant, gentle curves for trucks. [S26]
- Use collector spines + local spurs; avoid sending trucks left-turning across busy arterials into depots (prefer one-way loops). See TRAFFIC_GUIDE.md.
- Connect cargo train terminals to the **outside rail network**; unconnected stations are just storage. [TRAFFIC / Timeister]

## Ports (Bridges & Ports DLC)
- Treat ports as mini-cities: looping/cul-de-sac internals, berths, bulk storage, warehouses at orderly angles. [S03]
- CPP studied Charleston, LA/Long Beach, Milwaukee, Rotterdam — efficiency, land reclamation mindset, rail separated from highway where possible, services mixed with private uses. [S03]
- Plan rail into the port as it levels; add signature buildings sparingly for life. [S03]

## Pollution
- Check wind; keep air/ground pollution off housing. [S01]
- Do not put industry on drinking aquifers / groundwater you need for water towers or geothermal. [S01]

## Claude checklist
1. Survey resource overlays on unlocked Barney tiles.
2. Propose one chain (e.g. forestry or grain) with roads + optional rail.
3. Ask before buying resource tiles or demolishing grown industry.
4. After builds: check employees, truck paths, and cargo station connectivity.
