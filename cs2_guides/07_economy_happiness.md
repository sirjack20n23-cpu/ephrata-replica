# 07 — Economy, taxes, rent & happiness

## Budget rhythm
1. Grow taxable buildings and local production. [S01]
2. Add only the services you need. [S17]
3. Use small parks/leisure to help buildings level and recover tax after service costs. [S01]
4. Adjust taxes/fees gently; re-check demand and happiness. [S01][S17]

## Taxes & fees
- CPP observation (Economy 2.0 guide): residential often prefers ~**10%** and may tolerate ~**12%**; other sectors differ. Treat as a starting hint — verify in your patch. [S01]
- Service fees exist for electricity / water / sewage — part of early mandatory costs. [S17]
- Extreme max-tax cities can be forced to work in experiments (Erdgeist) but hurt realistic growth — **do not** do this on Barney unless the user wants a challenge. [S24]
- Loans unlock/expand with milestones — borrow for productive infrastructure, not cosmetics. [S01]

## Local production = money
- Local goods → cheaper prices → factory profits → richer cims → more tax. [S01]
- TotalXclipse focuses early unlocks / industry paths that accelerate income (order is version-sensitive). [S14]

## Tile buying
- Do not buy every neighboring tile early just to "unlock the map." Tiles cost money and stretch services. Expand when economy and demand justify it. [S17]

## High rent (Ollotize order)
When a building complains about high rent: [S25]
1. Inspect residents (education / income).
2. If poorly educated → more education + higher-paying jobs (zone commercial/office/industry).
3. Build more **low-rent residential**.
4. If land value spiked from parks/services, carefully thin amenities.
5. Industry wants cheap land — do not over-service industrial parks.
6. Some periods had buggy high-rent spam — still try balance fixes first.

## Skilled labor shortages
- Check the workplace employees tab (education mix + open positions). [S25]
- Mild gap: raise education budget slightly. [S25]
- Serious gap: Education info view — add schools where eligible students exceed capacity. [S25]

## Happiness / wellbeing
- Shorter transit trips help. [S15]
- Leisure coverage citywide matters; small parks are efficient. [S01]
- Reduce noise (buffers, electric buses in suburbs, hierarchy). [S27][S01]

## Claude checklist
1. Read budget, demand, and top complaints before proposing tax changes.
2. List exact tax/fee clicks for the **user** if the bridge cannot set them.
3. Prefer production + parks + education fixes over punitive taxes.
