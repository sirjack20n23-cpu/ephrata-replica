# 05 — Utilities

## Before any growth district
Confirm **power + water + sewage** coverage. Garbage soon after. [S01][S17]

## Power
- Roads carry underground power once connected through the network; early cities can import via outside connection through a transformer. [S01]
- Check wind overlay before wind turbines — weak wind sites never improve. Prefer reliable plants (e.g. coal) when wind is poor; coal needs fuel deliveries. [S01]
- **Bottlenecks**: too much load through one street/transformer path. Fix by placing **additional transformer stations** and feeding them with power lines so power enters the local grid in multiple places. [S31]
- Large plants (nuclear-scale examples) still need multiple transformers. [S31]
- Geothermal needs groundwater — protect those aquifers from industry pollution. [S01]
- Creative siting: waterfront + rail-served plants read realistic (OVERCHARGED EGG). [S23]
- Full electricity planning and build procedure: [POWER_GRID_GUIDE.md](../POWER_GRID_GUIDE.md) with [blueprint](../power_grid_blueprint.png). Verify live MW/MWh ratings; do not use batteries as transformers.

## Water
- Cannot import water like early power; build pumps/towers and pipe under roads. [S01]
- Prefer clean water sources; keep intakes upstream of sewage. [S01][S28]
- Groundwater towers: do not industrialize on top of the aquifer you drink from. [S01]

## Sewage
- Basic outlet is cheap but polluting (water + some air/noise). Put it distant / industrial / downstream. [S28]
- Treatment plants clean much more sewage but cost far more to build and run — unlock/budget carefully (exact figures are version-sensitive). [S28]
- Optional late game: recycle sewage toward power/water (Erdgeist experiments). [S24]

## Garbage
- Build collection/processing before trash overflows; raise garbage budget if service fails. [S01][S17]
- Plan landfill/incinerator expansion before you are desperate.

## Telecom
- Lower early priority than water/power/sewage; add when unlocked and complaints appear. (Thin transcript coverage — verify in UI.)

## Claude checklist
1. On new tiles/districts: utilities first, zoning second.
2. On power warnings: check transformers/paths, not only plant size.
3. Ask before expensive treatment or mega power projects.
